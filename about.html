About Me

Hi, I'm Alfie — a motion designer who likes making complicated things simple to watch.

Portrait photo — assets/img/portrait.jpg

I'm a motion designer with experience across junior to middleweight roles, producing animation, product renders, and branded content for clients ranging from local organisations to international brands. My work spans agency campaigns, studio pipelines, and live events, giving me a well-rounded understanding of creative workflows.

I studied Visual Effects and Motion Graphics at the University of South Wales, graduating with a 2:1, and I've since worked across three agencies — DW Studio, R4 Advertising, and CR Studio — on projects for clients including Dyson, Cardiff City FC, and Zimmer Biomet.
