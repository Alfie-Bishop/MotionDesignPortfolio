About Me

Hi, I'm Alfie — a motion designer who likes making complicated things simple to watch.

Over the last four years I've worked across three agencies, helping brands explain, launch, and advertise their products through motion. My work sits across explainer video, large-format anamorphic advertising, 3D texturing & animation, and video editing — usually wearing all of those hats within a single project.

I studied towards a degree in Animation & Motion Design before moving agency-side, and I've since worked with 15+ clients across finance, health, sport, energy, and beauty.
